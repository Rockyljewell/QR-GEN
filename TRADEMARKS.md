# Name and logo policy

The QRGen code is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The license covers the code,
not the project's identity: the QRGen name, the QRGEN wordmark and the logo identify the original
project created by [Rockyljewell](https://github.com/Rockyljewell), and section 6 of the license
does not grant permission to use them.

## You're welcome to

- Say that your product uses, is built on or is compatible with QRGen.
- Use the unmodified name and logo to link to the project, for example in a "Powered by QRGen" badge.
- Fork the code, change it and publish your fork under a different name.
- Write about QRGen, teach it and make videos about it.

## Please don't

- Publish a fork or a derived product under the name QRGen, a confusingly similar name, or with the
  QRGen logo.
- Present your product as the official QRGen, or suggest that Rockyljewell endorses it, without
  written permission.
- Remove the copyright headers or the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file. The license requires you to keep them.

Not sure whether your use is fine? Open an issue at https://github.com/Rockyljewell/QR-GEN/issues.
